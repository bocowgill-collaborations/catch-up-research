# Axiom audit for completed margins and causal terminal repayment

Every public theorem introduced in the three research modules is checked transitively.
Only the standard axioms are allowed. In particular, no sorryAx or conjecture assumption
may enter these proofs. This file is an executable check, not an extra axiom.

open Lean Elab Command in
run_cmd do
  let declarations := #[
    ``CatchUp.GamePosition.completion_sign,
    ``CatchUp.GamePosition.completed_margin_even,
    ``CatchUp.GamePosition.completed_win_margin_ge_two,
    ``CatchUp.completed_margin_transfer,
    ``CatchUp.erase_two_pairs_even,
    ``CatchUp.GamePosition.signed_picks_balance,
    ``CatchUp.GamePosition.completed_margin_ledger,
    ``CatchUp.GamePosition.PickTrace.effective_parity,
    ``CatchUp.GamePosition.PickTrace.remaining_card_le,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.phase_closed,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.phase_terminal_margin,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.phase_after_trace,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.greedy_terminal_margin,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.shadow_first,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.shadow_second,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.shadow_trace,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.shadow_forced_reply,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.repaired_margin_bound,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.greedy_legal_exists,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.phase_can_avoid_loss,
    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.actual_can_avoid_loss,

    ``CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.copied_suffix_margin_obstruction,

  for declaration in declarations do

    for axiomName in actual do

    logInfo m!"Verified axioms for {declaration}: {actual}"
