## Initial proof blueprint

one member; defender must remove its mate (any other response loses immediately).

Catch-Up: first test persistence of adversarial deletion choices under adaptive
completed turns. Any branching count must count distinct remaining sets/deficits/
actual movers, not move histories. A fallback first-two-level lower bound can use
the fact that swapped opening/reply pairs have opposite actual movers, preventing
the apparent merging of identical remaining sets.
