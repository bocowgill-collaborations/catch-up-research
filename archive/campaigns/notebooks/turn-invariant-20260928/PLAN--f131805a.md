## Selected mechanism: prepaid gap reserve

The older matching and queue failures rule out unconditional pairing. This
attempt instead asks whether explicit inequalities can make pairing causally
valid throughout play, and whether the intact interval can initialize them.
This is an analysis of a precisely restricted mechanism, not a revival of the
unconditional matching conjecture.

At an opponent-turn boundary, partition the remaining support into disjoint
unequal positive pairs. Let D be the sum of their absolute gaps and m a lower
bound for every paired counter. Let L be the protected player's current lead.
Candidate inequalities: D <= L and L + D <= m. They are meant to pay every
possible unfavorable pair orientation while preventing the opponent from
preparing multiple paired counters in a turn. The protected response is the
mate, not an outcome lookup. At the protected phase retain the actual selected
counter's still-available mate and the resulting deficit.

The one permitted refinement adds an arbitrary unpaired pool B of total W,
treated conservatively as available to the opponent. Use

    D + W <= L,       L + D + W <= m.

A proposed macro is: opponent prepares any subset of B of weight t and then
selects one paired counter x; protected selects its mate z. Set delta=z-x.
The intended update is

    L' = L - t + delta, D' = D - |delta|, W' = W - t.

The two slacks change by delta+|delta| and delta-|delta|-2t respectively.
This must be justified by the actual turn inequalities, including exhaustion
of the pair reserve and ties. No retrospective assignment of ownership is used.

## Blueprint and decisive check, before new code

1. Verify the completed-turn API and terminal conventions (done by inspection).
2. Prove or refute the two-phase reserve inequalities under arbitrary opponent
   preparation. Do not replace legality with bookkeeping.
3. Check initialization after an arbitrary legal first pick a from Icc 3 N.
   Then L=a-1. Permit any pairing, any unpaired pool, and any admissible m.
4. If initialization fails, derive the exact parametric obstruction. Do not
   turn the missing entry strategy into an assumption or create another phase.
5. Only formalize a central structural theorem if it materially clarifies this
   route. Do not formalize generic invariant-implies-safety scaffolding.

Why interval geometry matters: each integer strictly below the paired minimum
must either be the one selected opening or belong to the unpaired pool. This
may impose a reserve cost incompatible with the opening lead. This is a
checkable algebraic condition, not a game-value claim.

No computational falsification is currently needed. The first decision is the
symbolic compatibility of the reserve inequalities with interval initialization.
