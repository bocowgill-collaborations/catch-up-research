# What the experiments actually tell us

These are **retained observations from earlier bounded runs**, not new computations or certificates. The linked current Lean sources identify which diagnostics were subsequently proved. Full historical accounts are in the [notebook](../archive/research-notes/README.md), with [raw numerical records](../computation/observations/README.md) and their hashes. No listed sparse support disproves the interval conjecture.

| Experiment | Finding and scope | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seven-pair recursive mate rule | 594 supports and every first opposing pick passed the initial-response test. Expanding the next response round exposed two failures of the same recursion; [SevenPairTest](../CatchUp/SevenPairTest.lean) proves an unbounded embedding of one failure. | Initial-response success is not full strategy correctness. |
| Whole-turn balancing | The seven-/nine-pair test retained 3,908 opponent turns. Most had no immediately balanced reply. [WholeTurn](../CatchUp/WholeTurn.lean) proves that for every initial N>=7, P2 cannot take its entire draw quota in its first turn. | Absence of immediate balance does not imply loss; multi-turn defense remains possible. |
| Triangle repair and four-pair orphans | The three-core-pair defense is proved in [ReserveTriangle](../CatchUp/ReserveTriangle.lean). Testing four-pair orphans found a winning equal-deficit example; [FourOrphan](../CatchUp/FourOrphan.lean) certifies it. [NearRepair](../CatchUp/NearRepair.lean) closes that particular reached decision for all even q>=42. | The other orphan families and arbitrary larger-pair recursion do not follow. |
| Full strategic gap premise | Through ambient N=16, 95 exact blocks in 92 distinct gap cases met the retained winning-opening premise; no tested successor lost. The earlier geometric diagnostic fails that premise. | No uniform strategic gap-closing theorem; the premise cannot be removed. |
| Anchor-reflection tests | The original 1,536 even-tail shadows were all nonlosing, so none tested the proposed implication with a true loss antecedent. A held-out batch independently checked 202 shadows, again with no loss. | These are not successful nonvacuous tests of reflection. |
| Two-anchor double loss | A fixed mutation search stopped at its first simultaneous deficit-1/deficit-3 loss after 11,149 cases; 7,592 planned cases were unprocessed. [TwoAnchorDoubleLossObstruction](../CatchUp/TwoAnchorDoubleLossObstruction.lean) certifies the witness and a winning high shadow. | It refutes the unconditional auxiliary, not the actual high-shadow-loss conditional. No global minimality claim. |
| Actual interval entrance 4 | For E_N={4,7} union [9,N], deficit 1, N in {11,12,15,16,19,20}, all six roots drew computationally. First pick 4 fails at N=12; only 7 is safe there. All 51 resulting opponent endpoints were independently replayed. | A fixed entrance policy is false; the general E_N nonloss question remains. |
| Actual successor reply | On the same 51 endpoints, a prescribed adjacent/successor policy lost nine times. [IntervalSuccessorReplyObstruction](../CatchUp/IntervalSuccessorReplyObstruction.lean) certifies the N=15, opponent-11 diagnostic. | A drawn endpoint need not permit the nearest available counter as a safe reply. |
| Consecutive three-anchor cores | For B_N={2,3,4} union [9,N] on those six Ns, choosing 2 was safe against all 57 opponent replies in the retained exact experiment; every friendly choice at the endpoints was recorded. | A uniform theorem that choosing 2 works, or that adjacent replies suffice. |
| Equality c=5 test | All 21 tested high-shadow values were WIN or DRAW. The proposed conditional repair was never entered. | Neither evidence that the repair works nor proof that its loss antecedent is impossible. |
| Minimum-two mutation | The first losing support appeared at case 40 of a fixed 199-case list; 159 were unprocessed. [UnitGuardObstruction](../CatchUp/UnitGuardObstruction.lean) later certified all twelve first-pick losses. | The earlier small-cardinality SMT UNSAT results do not prove the refuted uniform principle. |
| Alternating-tail geometry | A 229-input batch stopped after 212 completed cases at a sparse loss. A later compact witness has a balanced allocation but still loses; [AlternatingTailObstruction](../CatchUp/AlternatingTailObstruction.lean) certifies it. | Alternating parity and even tail mass do not replace actual interval geometry or strategic premises. |

The saved files distinguish incomplete lists, skipped parity cases and unqueried targets. An unqueried target recorded as null is not a draw. Historical solver timing and memory refer to those runs; they are not newly measured publication benchmarks. Mathematical assurance comes from the current proved evaluator/checker and actual checked instances, not from a report's use of the word “verified.”

The [current route guide](research-routes.md) explains which hypotheses remain relevant. New experiments should challenge a specific surviving claim, retain every relevant opponent response and save the smallest diagnostic found, rather than silently patching a fixed policy with exceptions.
