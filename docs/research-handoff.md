## Exact mathematical objectives

Let `I_N={1,…,N}` and `Q_N(b)=Position(I_N\{1,b},b−1)`. The sufficient closure
route still needs **all lower-ray nonloss obligations together with the first-pair
draw** `Q_N(2)=Position({3,…,N},1)`. First-pair nonloss alone does not provide
the draw, and finitely many upper endpoints do not cover the interior. See
[PairDeletion](../CatchUp/PairDeletion.lean),
[LowerRaySuccessor](../CatchUp/Earlier/LowerRaySuccessor.lean), and
[the opening guide](openings.md) for formal hypotheses.
The uniform research target is for eligible N with even triangular total;
first-pair draw is not asserted for arbitrary odd-total intervals. Small and
degenerate cases must be handled separately when assembling the main theorem.

The newer core route leaves `{4,7}∪[9,N]` at deficit one under the original
losing high-shadow `{2,3,4,7}∪[9,N]` at deficit eight. Interior reentry and equality
issues also involve `{3,4}∪[9,N]`. Top-six exclusion alone is not infinite descent:
supports and required hypotheses change after reduction. A uniform, causal,
adversarially closed transfer or induction preserving interval geometry and those
hypotheses remains the key mathematical need.

## Do not repeat these shortcuts

| Shortcut | Exact obstacle / obligation |
| --- | --- |
| Copy common counters in adjacent-hole states | Certified first repair requires different picks U:2 and V:5. |
| Discard first-pair interval geometry | A sparse even-effective-total support with minimum 3 loses while its deficit-two shadow wins. |
| Treat shifted replies as tied winning openings | Shifted two-cycles exist; different residuals and player switches matter. Closed-walk existence proves no acyclicity. |
| Initialize the specified prepaid reserve for large N | Its envelope permits only N=7, opening 3, in the stated range; other invariants are not excluded. |
| Compare different exact-sum residuals using uniqueness | Uniqueness applies to one tied position, not different residual sets or positive-deficit parents. |
| Plan a multi-pick reply without checking prefixes | A turn ends at its first catch, including equality. Raw update identities are not legal strategies. |
| Infer a universal proof-length barrier from full certificates | The exponential bound concerns a specified full-DAG language, not all symbolic proofs. |

for witnesses, exact scopes, and what stronger arguments would still need.
